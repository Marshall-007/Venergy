Privacy notice

Last updated: 27 October 2026

V Energy is a trading name of Vees Capital (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us"). We respect your privacy and handle your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). This notice explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address and suburb.
  • Information about your enquiry, such as the service you're interested in, your preferred call time, your approximate electricity bill and any notes you add.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit the site, such as your device type and pages viewed.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry, schedule your call and arrange a site assessment.
  • To prepare quotes and deliver, support and maintain your solar system.
  • To keep records required by law, such as compliance certificates.

We only contact you about your enquiry and our services. We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Website forms are delivered to our team by email through a third-party form service (FormSubmit). If you choose to view our live Google reviews, that content is loaded from a reviews provider. We may share information with trusted suppliers, such as equipment manufacturers for warranty claims, only when needed to serve you.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. You may also object to us processing it, or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, email sales@venergy.co.za or call 010 824 5582.
