How much does a solar system cost?

Every installation is priced according to its scope. As a guide, our standard packages start from R25 000 for a 2.5 kVA backup system, R56 000 for 3 kVA, R116 000 for 5 kVA and R210 000 for a 10 kVA whole-home system. Your final price depends on your usage, roof, cabling, battery needs and location, and we confirm it in writing after a free site assessment.

Will solar keep my lights on during load-shedding?

Yes. A hybrid inverter with battery storage switches over automatically when the grid goes down, so your essential appliances keep running. During the day your panels keep powering your home and recharging the batteries.

How much can I save on electricity?

Savings depend on your system size, usage pattern and tariff. Our Smart Solar Advisor gives an instant estimate, and we confirm exact figures after your free site assessment.

How long does installation take?

Most residential systems are installed in one to two days once the design is approved and the equipment is on site. Larger commercial projects are scheduled to minimise disruption to your business.

Do I need approval from my municipality or Eskom?

Grid-tied systems must be registered with your municipality or Eskom as a small-scale embedded generator (SSEG). We guide you through the requirements and provide the documentation you need, including a Certificate of Compliance (CoC).

How long do solar panels, inverters and batteries last?

Tier-1 solar panels typically carry 25-year performance warranties. Quality lithium (LiFePO4) batteries are rated for thousands of cycles, often 10 years or more, and hybrid inverters usually come with 5- to 10-year manufacturer warranties.

Can I expand my system later?

Yes. We design systems to be modular, so you can add panels or batteries as your needs or budget grow.

Do you offer maintenance and repairs?

Yes. We offer health checks, fault-finding, panel cleaning, repairs and upgrades, plus emergency call-outs if your system goes down.

Which areas do you cover?

Our head office is in Northriding, Randburg, and we install across all nine provinces: Gauteng, the Western Cape, KwaZulu-Natal, the Free State, the Eastern Cape, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, the Northern Cape and North West. We also take on projects in neighbouring countries. Just ask.
